# Homework 3 -- Monetary and Fiscal Policies in RANKs and HANKs models

This homework is based on the mix of different articles of the literature and uses the perturbation methods developed in SSJ paper -- Auclert, Bardóczy, Rognlie, Straub -- and/or the higher order method by BBEG Bhandari, Bourany, Evans, Golosov.

The goal is to be able to simulate New Keynesian models (à la Woodford-Gali) and heterogeneous agents New Keynesian model and understand the policy implications for fiscal and monetary policies.
